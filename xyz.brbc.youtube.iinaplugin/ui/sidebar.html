                      Settings & data

                      Transparency depends on IINA’s sidebar background.

                      English searches use resilient Japanese translation and Japanese/Japan discovery context. Home keeps fetching until it has a useful set of Japanese recommendations, then gently re-ranks them using your local recent viewing history. Home topic chips filter the already-loaded personalized feed instead of running searches. Subscriptions stay complete and language-neutral. This cannot verify spoken audio.

                      Gently hides obvious gameplay, pranks, music videos and sports highlights. Keeps ambiguous titles and educational analysis. Works with Japanese mode; does not judge source reliability.

                      Hides titles with phrases such as “you won’t believe”, “shocking truth” and “必見”, or excessive punctuation. This is a title heuristic, not an AI-quality detector.

                      Videos with unknown duration are kept. Filters affect Feed, Search and Related; Recent stays available.

                      Use “Hide channel” on a video, or edit these lists. Remove a name to show it again. Names can change and are not unique.

                      Remembers the latest visit to up to 5,000 videos from this version onward. Does not record playback progress or send history to Google.

                      Topic matching is conservative and may miss relevant videos. For window resizing between videos, check IINA’s “Resize window to fit video size” setting.

                      Backups contain local channels, history and these settings. Import merges a plugin backup or a subscriptions CSV with Channel Id and Channel Title columns. Login credentials are excluded. These files preserve your data; direct import into a Google account is not provided.
